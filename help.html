From the lab folder to your report

You build the report by editing the lab do-files to fit your own question, in a copy of your lab folder. You do not start from scratch, you do not add new files next to the lab’s, and you do not need anything the lab question needed but yours does not.

1. Copy. Make a copy of the whole project folder (Mac: select it and press Cmd+D; Windows: copy and paste) and rename the copy na310g-<your code>. Leave the lab folder as it is, to look back at.

2. Clear out the labs, in the copy. Delete:

  • lab1-catchup, lab2-catchup, check_lab1.do, check_lab2.do, ai_task_lab1 and ai_task_lab2;
  • everything inside data/processed, output/logs and output/tables (old logs, tables and data files: master.do rebuilds them from your own data);
  • in data/raw, the course dataset na310g_data_course.dta. Put your own na310g_data_<your code>.dta there instead, unchanged.

Keep master.do, the folder dofiles, README and report_checklist.

3. Adapt each do-file to your question. The same files, in the same order; change what differs:

  • 1_load_check.do: the use line reads your own file.
  • 2_sample_vars.do: your industry (B1) and geography (B2), your outcome (B3), and the sample steps for your variables (B5).
  • 3_descriptives.do: your variables, and the table’s title and note.
  • 4_regressions.do: your outcome in every regression and in the table. Keep one table, the three-column one (pooled OLS, fixed effects, random effects); delete the Lab 1 table if it is still there.
  • 5_tests_iv.do: the tests with your outcome. The IV part (G) only if you argue for an instrument.
  • In each file, update the header (PURPOSE, CREATED) and make the comments say why you do each step.

4. The occupation file is optional. Your research question needs no merge: your outcome, ai_firm and the required controls are all in your own dataset. You need na310g_occupations_ssyk2012.dta only if you use an occupation variable, for example as an instrument (for a VG). If you do not, delete block B4 (the merge) in 2_sample_vars.do, block G in 5_tests_iv.do, and the occupation file in data/raw.

5. Run everything from the copy. Close Stata, then double-click the copy’s master.do: it sets $root to the folder Stata opens in, so if Stata is still open from the lab folder, your files write there. master.do must run from top to bottom without an error. Its log, with the verification line, goes in your appendix.

Getting the most out of it

  1. Run your do-file from the top first. Many problems disappear when the file runs in one go.
  2. Bring the smallest piece of code that shows the problem, and the error message.
  3. Say what you expected to see before you ran it. That is usually where the misunderstanding is.
