What this part examines

  • Computer labs (A001, compulsory): attend both labs. Participation is required before you can submit the report. If you cannot attend one of them for special reasons, contact Magnus Lodefalk in advance.
  • Individual report (A004, 3.5 credits): a quantitative analysis of an economic question with panel data in Stata, written up as a short research report. Instructions are handed out at the first lab.

The written exam (A005, 4 credits) is Daniela Andrén’s; see the course guide.
