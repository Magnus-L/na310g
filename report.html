The report

Your individual report (A004): the instructions, an example, and the checklist.

Instructions (PDF)

In short

  • Deadline: Sunday 8 November 2026, 16:00. One PDF by e-mail to magnus.lodefalk@oru.se, subject NA310G: Assignment, file named with your personal code and -FINAL (for example K7Q2MX-FINAL.pdf).
  • Resubmission: a report that needs a revision gets written feedback. The revised report is due Sunday 29 November 2026, 16:00, sent the same way.
  • Length: at most 2,500 words, plus an appendix with your do-files, final log, the verification line and the AI declaration.
  • Your question and data are personal: you download them with your code (page Your data, before Lab 1).
  • Added controls: one or two of your own, motivated by theory or the article and tested jointly (testparm, Lab 2), are recommended for a VG but not required. The quality of the reasoning decides a VG.
  • AI: to learn, never to do the analysis or write the report. See AI in the labs and the report.
  • Where you work: in a copy of your lab folder, editing the lab do-files to fit your question. Step by step, with what to delete; the occupation file is needed only for an instrument.

The instructions are the authoritative text: everything about the data, your research question, the template, the formalities, and what earns a pass and a VG.

Readings

The reading list from the instructions, with links. Starred (*): read carefully, and summarise one of them in your report. Hash (#): browse. The rest are optional. Every link is free to open; where the journal version sits behind a paywall, the link goes to the working-paper version.

  • *Engberg, E., Görg, H., Lodefalk, M., Javed, F., Längkvist, M., Monteiro, N. P., Kyvik Nordås, H., Pulito, G., Schroeder, S. and Tang, A. (2024). AI Unboxed and Jobs: A Novel Measure and Firm-Level Evidence from Three Countries. IZA Discussion Paper No. 16717. PDF · IZA page. The source of the AI exposure measure, DAIOE, in your data.
  • *Engberg, E., Hellsten, M., Javed, F., Lodefalk, M., Sabolová, R., Schroeder, S. and Tang, A. (2025). Artificial Intelligence, Hiring and Employment: Job Postings Evidence from Sweden. Applied Economics Letters, 33(16), 3138–3143. Working paper (Ratio WP 380), PDF · journal version.
  • #Hellsten, M. (2025). Returns to AI Skills. Manuscript (Ratio Working Paper No. 378). PDF.
  • #SCB. AI-användning i företag 2025 (the most recent version of the report earlier called Näringslivets användning av AI). Statistics Sweden. 2025 report · 2024 edition.
  • Felten, E., Raj, M. and Seamans, R. (2021). Occupational, Industry, and Geographic Exposure to Artificial Intelligence: A Novel Dataset and Its Potential Uses. Strategic Management Journal, 42(12), 2195–2217. Article (open access).
  • Engberg, E., Koch, M., Lodefalk, M. and Schroeder, S. (2025). Artificial Intelligence, Tasks, Skills and Wages: Worker-Level Evidence from Germany. Research Policy, 54(8), 105285. Article (open access).

  • Lodefalk, M. (2024). Artificiell intelligens och jobben. Ratio. A book in Swedish, in the library and bookshops. Ratio page.
