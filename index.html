What this part examines

  • Computer labs (A001, compulsory): attend at least two of the three labs, preferably Lab 1 and Lab 2. Participation is required before you can submit the report. If you cannot attend for special reasons, contact Magnus Lodefalk in advance.
  • Individual report (A004, 3.5 credits): a quantitative analysis of an economic question with panel data in Stata, written up as a short research report of at most 2,500 words (tables, references and appendix not counted). The instructions are on the page The report.

The written exam (A005, 4 credits) belongs to the other part of the module; see the course guide on Blackboard.

Grades for the report: Fail (U), Pass (G) or Pass with Distinction (VG); exchange students also get an ECTS grade (A–F). You get written feedback after the deadline. A report graded conditionally passed can be revised: one major and one minor revision.

Before Lab 1 (Thu 22 Oct): five steps, about two hours

  1. Install Stata (free for students at the School of Business), start it once and make sure it is up to date. The licence and the installation instructions are on Blackboard; the same information was e-mailed to all School of Business students earlier this year. For technical problems, e-mail itsupport@oru.se.
  2. Watch the five videos (about 75 minutes in all): 1. Welcome, and how to pass the report; 2. setting up Stata, the project folder and master.do; 3. your first do-file; 4. append and merge; 5. reading regress output. Videos 4 and 5 (and 6 to 8 later) run on their own small example data, not on the do-files in the project folder, so you only need to watch them. In the labs we do the same steps on the course data.
  3. Read the instructions for the report, so you know in Lab 1 what the labs build towards. The rules for using AI apply from Lab 1.
  4. Download the project folder (one zip) and unzip it into Documents, not OneDrive, Dropbox or iCloud. This is where all your lab and report work lives.
  5. Download the data, unzip, and put the files themselves (not the folders they come in), unchanged, in the folder data/raw/ inside your project folder; never edit them there. For the labs: the course dataset, whose two files na310g_data_course.dta and na310g_occupations_ssyk2012.dta both go in data/raw/. For the report: your own dataset and research question, with your personal code from Blackboard, on the page Your data.

Bring your laptop with Stata and the project folder to Lab 1. Fall behind or miss part of a lab? The project folder has a folder lab1-catchup with the lab’s code. If you downloaded the project folder before 21 October, download the Lab 1 catch-up, unzip it and put the folder lab1-catchup at the top of your project folder, next to master.do. The data page explains the course data, and how to get Serrano free for your thesis.

  • Before Lab 2 (Mon 26 Oct): 6. which test after which command; 7. panel data, fixed and random effects, and the Hausman test; 8. instrumental variables, the first stage and weak instruments (also before the IV lecture).
  • Before Lab 3 (Wed 28 Oct): 9. from log to report: tables, the appendix and handing in.

Lab 2 catch-up: if you downloaded the project folder before 26 October, download the Lab 2 catch-up, unzip it and put the folder lab2-catchup at the top of your project folder, next to master.do.
